# PitchLens demo narration

Spoken script for [the 1:58.7 submission video](pitchlens-demo-final.mp4).
Narration is synthesized with Microsoft MAI-Voice-2.1 (voice `en-US-Grant`)
through Azure AI Speech on the project's Microsoft Foundry resource. The source
of truth is [tools/narration.json](tools/narration.json); the matching
subtitles are in [pitchlens-demo-final.srt](pitchlens-demo-final.srt).

Times are in the final video. The first 9.27 s are the intro card; after that,
video time is screen-recording time plus 9.27 s. Playback and interface actions
continue during the pauses. The last line ends at 1:57.4 and the video fades out
at 1:58.7.

## Continuous reading copy

PitchLens: explainable match intelligence, built on Microsoft Foundry and narrated by Microsoft's MAI Voice.

Statistics tell you what happened. PitchLens explains why a moment matters, with evidence you can replay.

Every team, player and event is synthetic. A seeded simulator generates the match, so the same settings always play out the same way.

A foul, a free kick, a blocked shot, and Harbor City take the lead. Each insight uses only evidence up to that moment.

Then another foul gives Northvale a penalty, and they equalize.

Tactics shows patterns measured in code. Counts describe actions, not pressure or intent.

A Foundry model writes the explanation. Facts are built in code, the model selects and phrases them, and a separate verifier checks the answer first.

Switch to Analyst for a fresh live request.

More precise detail, from exactly the same evidence.

Every claim links to evidence you can replay.

Seek back before the penalty, and the later insight disappears. The model cannot read past the selected time.

Preferences focus moments and patterns on a favorite team or player. The evidence stays the same.

At full time, the recap brings together the result, the defining moments, and every explanation.

Automated checks reduce mistakes, but can't guarantee every claim is true. PitchLens: match intelligence you can check.
